Maharashtra Cooperative Societies Act, 1960: Winding Up and Liquidation

Section 107: Winding up of a society
A society may be wound up by the Registrar on his own motion or on the application of:
(a) a general body meeting of the society convened for that purpose by a resolution passed by a majority of not less than three-fourths of the members present and voting;
(b) the financing bank to which the society is indebted, where the society has failed to pay the dues within a period of six months from the date on which they became payable.

Section 108: Circumstances under which a society may be wound up
The Registrar may, after giving the society a reasonable opportunity of being heard, order the winding up of a society where:
(a) the society has not commenced working within one year from the date of its registration;
(b) the society has not held its annual general meeting for two consecutive years;
(c) the society has failed to submit its audited balance sheet for two consecutive years;
(d) the membership of the society has fallen below the minimum number prescribed for the registration of such a society;
(e) the society has, by a resolution passed by a majority of not less than three-fourths of its members, decided to wind up its affairs.

Section 109: Winding up order and its effects
On the making of a winding up order, the committee of the society shall cease to have any powers, except as may be necessary to carry out the directions of the Registrar or the liquidator.
The Registrar shall appoint a liquidator for the purpose of conducting the winding up proceedings, who shall take into his custody or under his control all assets and effects of the society.

Section 110: Powers and duties of the liquidator
The liquidator shall, within sixty days from the date of his appointment, submit a preliminary report to the Registrar showing the assets and liabilities of the society and the causes of its failure, if any. The liquidator shall then proceed to realize the assets of the society and discharge its liabilities in the order of priority prescribed by the rules.
Any surplus remaining after the discharge of all liabilities shall be distributed among the members in proportion to their share capital, or shall be transferred to the Co-operative Fund, as the Registrar may direct.
