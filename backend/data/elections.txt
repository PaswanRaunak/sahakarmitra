Maharashtra Cooperative Societies Act, 1960: Conduct of Elections

Section 73: Conduct of elections to the committee
(1) The election of the members of the committee of a society shall be conducted in the manner prescribed by the Registrar and, save as otherwise provided in the rules of the society, shall be by secret ballot.
(2) Every society shall, before holding the election of the members of its committee, prepare a list of voters and publish it on the notice board of the society at least fourteen days before the date fixed for the election.
(3) Any person whose name is included in the list of voters may contest the election by submitting a nomination paper duly proposed and seconded by two other voters of the same society.

Section 73B: Right to contest elections
A member of a society shall be eligible to contest the election of the committee if, on the date of filing nomination, the member:
(a) is not in arrears of any dues payable to the society;
(b) has not been held responsible for any loss caused to the society by an order passed under section 101;
(c) has not been subjected to any disqualification under the rules.
A member aggrieved by the rejection of a nomination may appeal to the Registrar within seven days of the rejection.

Section 73C: Election disputes
An election petition challenging the election of any member of the committee may be presented to the Registrar within thirty days from the date of declaration of the result. The Registrar may, after notice to the parties and inquiry, declare the election to be void or valid and, where void, direct a fresh election to be held for the seat in question.
