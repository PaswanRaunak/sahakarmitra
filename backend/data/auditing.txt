Maharashtra Cooperative Societies Act, 1960: Audit and Accounts

Section 81: Society to maintain books and accounts
Every society shall maintain such books and accounts as may be prescribed by the rules and shall have them audited annually by the auditor appointed under section 81A.
The books of accounts of the society shall be balanced and closed on the 30th day of June of each year or on such other date as may be specified in the by-laws of the society.

Section 81A: Appointment of auditor
At every annual general meeting, a society shall appoint an auditor qualified under section 81B to audit the accounts of the society for the next year. The auditor shall hold office until the conclusion of the next annual general meeting and shall be eligible for reappointment.
No auditor shall be eligible for appointment unless the auditor is on the panel of auditors maintained by the Registrar.

Section 81B: Qualifications of auditors
A person shall be qualified to be appointed as an auditor of a society if such person is:
(a) a chartered accountant within the meaning of the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949;
(b) a person who has served as an auditor of a co-operative society for not less than five years before the commencement of this Act.
No person who is a member of the committee or is in the employment of the society shall be appointed as an auditor of the society.

Section 83: Auditor's report
The auditor shall make a report to the society on the accounts examined by him and on every balance sheet and profit and loss statement laid before the society in general meeting during his tenure as auditor. The report shall state whether, in the auditor's opinion, the balance sheet and profit and loss statement show a true and fair view of the state of affairs of the society and whether the society has complied with the provisions of this Act and the rules made thereunder.
