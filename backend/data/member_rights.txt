Maharashtra Cooperative Societies Act, 1960: Member Rights and Liabilities

Section 24: Rights and privileges of members
A member of a society shall have the right to:
(a) attend and take part in general body meetings and vote on resolutions;
(b) inspect the registers, books and accounts of the society during office hours on any working day;
(c) receive a copy of the annual report, the audit memorandum, and the audited balance sheet;
(d) receive such dividend or bonus on the share capital held by the member as may be declared by the society.

Section 35: Withdrawal of a member
A member may withdraw from the society by giving at least one month's notice in writing to the Secretary, provided that the member has no liability to the society as borrower, guarantor, or otherwise.
The society shall pay to the withdrawing member the amount of his share capital and any other dues after deducting any amount outstanding against him, within a period not exceeding six months from the date of his withdrawal.

Section 36: Expulsion of a member
Notwithstanding anything contained in the by-laws, no member shall be expelled from a society except by a resolution passed by a majority of not less than three-fourths of the members present and voting at a general body meeting convened for that purpose. The member concerned shall be given a reasonable opportunity of being heard before the resolution is passed. A member expelled under this section may appeal to the Registrar within sixty days of the communication of the order of expulsion.

Section 28: Restrictions on loans
No society shall lend money to any person other than a member of the society, save as otherwise permitted by the Registrar. No society shall lend money to a member on the security of his own share in the society.
